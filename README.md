# Lakehead_Students_Shop
Problem: University students often need an easy and reliable way to buy and sell used items such as textbooks, electronics, furniture, and school supplies. Existing online marketplaces can be too broad, making it difficult for students to find relevant items from people within their campus community. 

Proposed System: Our project will develop a Java-based desktop Campus Marketplace application designed specifically for university students. The system will allow students to create accounts, post items for sale, and browse listings created by other users. 

Core Functionality: The application will include user registration and login, creation and management of listings, item images, search and filtering, favorites, buyer-seller messaging, user profiles, and the ability to mark items as sold. 

Value: The system will provide students with a simple and organized platform for buying and selling items within their university community. It can make transactions more convenient, help students save money, reduce waste by encouraging reuse, and provide a more focused alternative to general online marketplaces.
